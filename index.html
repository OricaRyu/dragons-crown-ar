The Dragon's Crown

An Augmented Reality Quest

A dragon has stolen the King's crown. Find the key, slay the beast, and bring the crown home.

  1. Search: tap the chests to find the golden key.
  2. Battle: turn around to find the dragon, then tap it to strike.
  3. Claim: take the crown and save the kingdom.

Allow camera & motion access when asked. Best in an open space.
